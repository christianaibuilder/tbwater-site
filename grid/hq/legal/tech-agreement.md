# Independent Technician Agreement — version 2026-11

This agreement is between [ENTITY], a Florida limited liability company doing business as [BRAND]
("Platform"), and the business or individual accepting it ("Technician").

## 1. Relationship

Technician is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates employment, partnership, agency, or
joint venture. Technician controls how, when, and by whom the work is performed; sets their own
prices; may accept or decline any job without penalty; may work for anyone else; and provides
their own tools, vehicle, parts sourcing, licenses, and insurance. Platform does not supervise the
work and does not guarantee any volume of jobs.

## 2. What Platform does

Platform operates a marketplace that introduces customers to independent technicians, relays job
details, hosts quotes and invoices, collects customer payments as Technician's limited payment
collection agent, and remits Technician's share. Platform is not a party to the service contract
between Technician and the customer.

## 3. Pricing and quotes

Technician sets all prices. Platform may prepare a draft quote from Technician's stated rates for
convenience; Technician may edit or reject any draft before it is sent. Quotes sent to customers
are Technician's quotes.

## 4. Fees

For each job paid through Platform, Platform retains a platform fee of **10% of the invoice total,
with a $25 minimum**, before remitting Technician's share. Card processing fees charged to the
customer are retained by Platform and do not reduce Technician's share. Fee changes take effect
with 30 days' written notice and apply only to jobs offered after the effective date.

## 5. Payment to Technician

Platform remits Technician's share after the customer's payment settles (bank payments can take
several business days). Platform may deduct amounts for customer refunds or chargebacks arising
from Technician's work, after notice to Technician and a reasonable opportunity to respond. No
payout is made until Platform has Technician's Form W-9 on file. Platform issues Form 1099-NEC
where required by law.

## 6. Insurance, licenses, and standards

Technician maintains commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence
and provides a certificate of insurance on request and on renewal; maintains all licenses,
registrations, and local business tax receipts required for the work; collects and remits any sales
tax due on its invoices; performs work in a good and workmanlike manner; and honors its quotes
unless the customer approves a change.

## 7. Customers introduced by Platform

For 12 months after a customer is introduced through Platform, Technician agrees to run that
customer's jobs through Platform. If Technician performs work for such a customer outside Platform
during that period, Technician owes Platform the platform fee that would have applied (the
"finder's fee"). This does not apply to customers Technician can show it served before the
introduction.

## 8. Customer information

Customer information is confidential, used only to perform the booked work, and never sold or
shared.

## 9. Indemnity and liability

Technician is responsible for the work and for any claims arising from it, and will indemnify
Platform against such claims. Platform's total liability to Technician under this agreement is
limited to fees Platform retained on the job in question in the prior 90 days.

## 10. Term

This agreement continues while Technician's account is active. Either party may end it with
written notice; jobs in progress are completed and paid under these terms. Platform may suspend an
account for lapsed insurance, repeated declines without reason, customer complaints, or breach.

## 11. Miscellaneous

Florida law governs. Disputes are resolved in the county where Platform is based. If part of this
agreement is unenforceable, the rest stands. This is the entire agreement and replaces earlier
versions. Acceptance is by checking the box at application; the version, date, and account are
recorded.
