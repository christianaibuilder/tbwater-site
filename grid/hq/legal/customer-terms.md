# Customer Terms — version 2026-11

**Template; attorney review recommended.** Replace [BRAND] and [ENTITY].

[BRAND] is operated by [ENTITY] ("we"). We connect you with independent technicians and handle
quotes, scheduling, and payment on their behalf.

1. **Who does the work.** Technicians on [BRAND] are independent businesses, not our employees.
   The technician performs the work, sets the price, carries the liability for it, and is the seller
   on your invoice. We screen technicians for insurance and experience but do not guarantee their
   work.
2. **Quotes.** A quote is the technician's offer. Parts are billed at the final invoiced amount.
   If the job changes, the technician asks you before doing more work.
3. **Payment.** Payment is due when you receive the invoice link. You may pay by bank transfer at the
   invoiced amount or by card with a disclosed processing fee. We collect payment as the technician's
   payment agent and pay the technician. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your full
   card or bank details.
4. **Recurring plans.** Maintenance plans may be paused or canceled with 7 days' notice. Visits
   already scheduled within the notice period remain billable.
5. **Cancellations.** Cancel a scheduled visit at least 24 hours ahead to avoid a trip fee.
6. **Problems with the work.** Tell us within 7 days. We will work with the technician to make it
   right. Our liability is limited to the fee we retained on the job.
7. **Contact.** By submitting a job you agree we and the assigned technician may call or email you
   about it. If you check the texting box, we may text you about the job; reply STOP to opt out.
8. **Privacy.** We collect your contact details, location, equipment details, photos, and job
   description to perform the service, and share them only with the assigned technician and our
   payment processor. Request deletion at any time by contacting us.
9. **Law.** Florida law applies.
