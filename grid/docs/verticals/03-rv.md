# Vertical 3 — Mobile RV technicians, Tampa Bay (plan; unlock at level 4)

Working brand: **Tampa Bay Mobile RV Techs**. Starter services and brands in
`VERTICAL_TEMPLATES.rv`.

## The market

Florida is one of the top three RV states by registrations and shipments (sources disagree on the
order; Florida took about 5.6% of 2025 shipments, second to California). Tampa Bay specifically:

- **Lazydays in Seffner** is among the largest RV dealerships in the country. Big dealers mean
  long service queues in season; RVs waiting weeks for a roof A/C or fridge is the standing
  complaint on every RV forum. That queue is the demand.
- **RV parks and 55-plus RV resorts** cluster in Zephyrhills and Dade City (Pasco, dozens of
  resorts), Thonotosassa, Riverview, Ruskin and Lithia (Hillsborough), Hudson and Spring Hill
  (Pasco/Hernando), with fewer in Pinellas. A park with 400 sites is 400 customers at one address.
- **Snowbirds** November to April: units arrive after a 1,200-mile drive and after months of
  storage. Pre-trip inspections, A/C, fridges, water heaters, slide-outs all fail on arrival.
- **Full-timers and remote workers** year-round, concentrated in the same resorts.

## Jobs and tickets

| Job | Typical ticket |
|---|---|
| Roof A/C repair or replacement (Dometic, Coleman-Mach) | $350 to $1,800 |
| Refrigerator (Norcold, Dometic absorption; 12V compressor) | $300 to $1,500 |
| Water heater (Suburban, Atwood), pumps, leaks | $250 to $900 |
| 12V electrical, converters, inverters, batteries, transfer switch | $300 to $1,200 |
| Slide-out motors, gear packs, seals; leveling systems (Lippert) | $400 to $1,500 |
| Roof reseal and leak tracing | $400 to $1,200 |
| Awning fabric and motors | $350 to $900 |
| Pre-trip or post-storage inspection | $200 to $350 |

Average ticket around $550 to $700. Mobile RV techs commonly charge $125 to $175 an hour plus a
trip fee of $75 to $150.

## Laws and credentials

- No Florida license to repair RV coach systems.
- **Propane:** servicing or installing LP gas systems and appliances requires an FDACS LP gas
  license, Category V for RV installers, with a qualifier who has at least a year of verifiable
  experience or a manufacturer certification (since July 2024). Fees run $200 to $600 depending on
  term. Rule for the platform: **propane work only by techs who hold the license; everyone else's
  profile excludes it, and the service list says so.** Appliances that burn propane (fridge, water
  heater, furnace) sit on the line; the licensed tech does the gas side.
- **RVTI certification** (RV Technical Institute): Level 1 entry, Level 2 diagnostic and repair,
  Level 3 specialties, Level 4 master. Not legally required, but Level 2 and above is the
  vetting bar that separates real techs from handymen. Collect it at application.
- Chassis work (engine, brakes on a motorhome) falls under the Motor Vehicle Repair Act like a car:
  FDACS registration and written estimates. Exclude chassis work from this vertical; it belongs to
  vertical 4.
- Parks require proof of insurance and often a vendor registration at the office.

## Competition

Dealer service departments (Lazydays, Camping World, General RV, La Mesa) with backlogs; a scattered
set of solo mobile techs found through Google and Facebook, many retiring; national franchises in
some metros. No dispatch marketplace with a park-by-park presence in Tampa Bay.

## Supply plan

Target 8 techs: 3 Pasco (Zephyrhills corridor), 3 Hillsborough, 1 Hernando, 1 Pinellas; at least
two with the LP license; all with RVTI Level 2 or equivalent experience. Sources: RVTI's
technician directory, RV park managers (they know every mobile tech who works their park),
Facebook RV groups, former dealer techs, and techs from vertical 1 with RV experience (marine
electrical and 12V skills transfer directly).

## Demand plan

- **Park offices are the channel.** Get on the referral list at 20 resorts: a one-page sheet,
  insurance certificate, a promise of same-week response. Offer the office a seasonal "arrival
  inspection" day where a tech is on site.
- Resort bulletin boards, newsletters, activity-director emails.
- Facebook: Florida snowbird groups, RV park groups, brand owner groups (Grand Design owners,
  Jayco owners) filtered to Tampa.
- Google: "mobile RV repair Zephyrhills", "RV AC repair Tampa", "RV refrigerator repair Pasco".
- Season launch in late October, before arrivals. Summer is slow; plan it as maintenance season
  for full-timers and roof work before hurricane season.

## Targets

First season (Nov to Apr): 8 techs, 25 paid jobs a month by February, average ticket $600, 20
parks on the referral list. Economics are similar to marine with less weather sensitivity.

## Risks

Propane liability if the licensing rule is not enforced; parts lead times on appliances; dealer
warranty work pulling customers back; seasonality concentrating revenue in six months.
