# Vertical 2 — Golf cart repair, Tampa Bay (plan; unlock at level 3)

Working brand: **Suncoast Cart Mechanics**. Starter services and brands load into HQ on unlock
(`hq/views/verticals.js` → `VERTICAL_TEMPLATES.golf_cart`).

## The market

**The Villages** is the famous one: estimates run from 60,000 to over 85,000 carts for about
150,000 residents (no official registry; these are press and industry estimates). It is 90 minutes
north of Tampa in Sumter, Lake and Marion counties. It is a separate metro with its own dealers and
should be a second territory later, not the first.

**Tampa Bay has its own cart towns**, closer and less served:

| Community | County | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sun City Center and Kings Point | Hillsborough | Cart-legal roads, tens of thousands of retirees, carts are primary transport. Local dealers: Parts 'n Carts, Eco Golf Carts |
| Valencia Lakes, Southshore Falls, Apollo Beach | Hillsborough | 55-plus and waterfront, high cart density |
| Wesley Chapel, Land O' Lakes (Del Webb Bexley, Esplanade) | Pasco | Newer 55-plus communities, lithium carts common |
| Timber Pines, Spring Hill, Hernando Beach | Hernando | Retiree density, few mobile techs |
| Tampa Bay Golf & Country Club, Lake Jovita | Pasco | Cart-centric |
| Dunedin, Pass-a-Grille, Anna Maria Island, beach towns | Pinellas, Manatee | Street-legal LSVs and rental fleets |
| Highland Lakes, On Top of the World | Pinellas | Large 55-plus communities |

**Why it fits the model.** Owners are older, phone-first, and value someone showing up at the
house. Shops are few and shop drop-off requires a trailer most owners don't have. Mobile is the
whole product.

## Jobs and tickets

| Job | Typical ticket |
|---|---|
| Battery pack replacement, lead-acid | $600 to $1,200 |
| Lithium conversion | $1,800 to $3,500 |
| Won't run: solenoid, controller, motor, F/R switch | $200 to $700 |
| Gas cart tune-up, starter, carburetor (Yamaha, Club Car, E-Z-GO) | $150 to $450 |
| Brakes, tires, suspension | $150 to $400 |
| Light kits, mirrors, turn signals (street-legal) | $250 to $600 |
| Annual service and safety check | $120 to $200 |

Average ticket is lower than marine, roughly $350 to $500, so the $25 minimum platform fee matters
and a recurring annual-service plan is the revenue lever. Lithium conversions are the big tickets
and the fastest-growing request.

## Laws that matter

- Florida HB 949 (2023): drivers under 18 need a learner's permit (15) or license (16); adults need
  government ID. Non-criminal infractions. Counties and cities may be stricter.
- Standard golf carts are not registered or titled; **low-speed vehicles (LSVs)** that go 20 to 25
  mph are titled, registered, insured, and need lights, mirrors, seat belts, VIN. Converting a cart
  to an LSV touches DMV rules; techs can do the equipment, owners handle the paperwork. Document
  that split in the service description.
- No state license to repair carts. Local business tax receipts apply as in marine.
- Lithium batteries: transport and disposal rules; techs must be set up for core returns and
  recycling. Insurance should not exclude lithium work; check certificates.
- Sales tax: same repair rules as marine (parts make the whole job taxable).

## Competition

Parts 'n Carts (Sun City Center dealer, Club Car, ICON, Denago), Eco Golf Carts (Sun City Center,
serves Hillsborough, Manatee, Sarasota), On The Go Mobile Golf Cart Repair (Tampa), Golf Cart
Services (Palm Harbor, mobile, Pinellas-wide), Velocity Custom Golf Cars (Wesley Chapel), Golf Carts
USA (Brandon, BBB F rating), Five Star Golf Carts (Clearwater/Largo), Golf Cars of Tampa Bay.
Dealers prioritize sales and their own brand's warranty work; independent mobile techs are scarce
in Pasco and Hernando. No marketplace or dispatch service exists for carts in the region.

## Supply plan

Target 8 techs: 3 in south Hillsborough (Sun City Center radius), 2 Pasco, 2 Pinellas, 1 Hernando.
Where: Facebook Marketplace ("golf cart repair" sellers), the dealers' former techs, battery
installers, small-engine and lawn equipment mechanics who want cart work (gas carts are small
engines), community bulletin boards in Sun City Center.

## Demand plan

- **Community channels beat Google here.** Sun City Center Community Association newsletter and
  bulletin boards, Kings Point clubs, HOA newsletters in Valencia Lakes and Del Webb Bexley,
  community Facebook groups and Nextdoor. A flyer in the community center produces more than a
  Google ad.
- Google Business Profile and a handful of pages: "golf cart repair Sun City Center", "golf cart
  batteries Wesley Chapel", "lithium conversion Tampa Bay".
- Annual service plan sold at every job: one visit a year, battery water, brakes, tires, lights.
  Recurring revenue and the reason techs stay.
- Season: snowbird season November to April is peak; carts that sat all summer in a garage fail in
  November.

## Targets

Unlock requirement reached in marine (level 3). First 60 days: 8 techs, 20 paid jobs, average
ticket $450, three annual plans sold. Decide after 90 days whether to add The Villages as its own
brand and territory.

## Risks

Lower tickets make the economics thinner; the minimum fee and plans fix that. Battery and lithium
work has real fire and disposal liability; verify insurance covers it. Dealers may pressure their
techs not to moonlight; recruit independents.
