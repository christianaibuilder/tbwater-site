## BoatGrid (the reference model)

- Site: https://boatgrid.site (Base44 app id created 2026-05-14; formerly "Boatly")
- Observed: free job posting; admin approves and assigns; techs pay a flat lead fee (default $25 in
  code) to unlock contact info, or BoatGrid collects the invoice and keeps 10%, paying 90% via
  QuickBooks vendor payments; recurring "management plans" with 7-day cancellation; AI invoice
  reading; auto-drafted email campaigns every 48 hours; voice assistant; push notifications.
- Public directory showed 0 technicians; testimonials are hardcoded; no indexed pages; no ads.
